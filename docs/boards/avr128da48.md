# An AVR128DA48 board

A self-built board for the 48-pin parts of three families - its
silkscreen reads "AVRxxx[DA|DB|EA]48 / ATmega4809 - REV 1.2" - fitted
here with an **AVR128DA48** (128 KB flash, 16 KB SRAM, signature
`1E 97 08`, `SYSCFG.REVID` 0x17), the DA of the `avrxt/` stratum's
bench. Design files: a repository in preparation.

- **Clock**: the DA has no crystal oscillator, so the board makes the
  high-frequency clock itself - a 24 MHz quartz and two single
  inverters - and feeds it to **PA0 as EXTCLK**; PA1 is then a GPIO.
  Solder jumpers choose among three arrangements (silkscreen "EXT CLK
  OPTS"): the quartz on PA0/PA1 for a DB's crystal oscillator, the
  inverter oscillator into PA0, or no clock and both pins free. A
  **32.768 kHz crystal on PF0/PF1**. Both measured running:
  [../avrxt/clkctrl.md](../avrxt/clkctrl.md) has how each is seen.
- **Supply**: jumper-selectable VDD (3.3 V, 5 V or an external supply
  through a Schottky diode) and a VDDIO2 selection that only a DB uses;
  measured at 5.07 V by the probe.
- **Console**: USB-C to a **CH340B** behind a digital isolator ("ISO
  USB"), reaching USART2 ALT1, **PF4 (TX) / PF5 (RX)**, through the "to
  UART" solder jumpers (the other position routes the two pins to the
  header); 460800 in the console apps. The CH340B carries a USB serial
  number written into its EEPROM with WCH's CH34xSerCfg - the tool's
  "disable the EEPROM configuration" box UNTICKED, or the chip ignores
  the configuration the tool reads back - so its console is addressed
  by id (`/dev/serial/by-id`) like a probe's. Never 0x57 at EEPROM
  offset 0x03: that byte sets the EEPROM's permanent write protection.
- **Probe**: any UPDI probe avrdude drives, on the board's 10-pin
  "ATMEL ICE AVR" connector - an Atmel-ICE on its AVR port
  ([../probes/atmel-ice.md](../probes/atmel-ice.md)) or a PICkit 4 in
  its AVR mode (the mechanisms in [../probes/README.md](../probes/README.md));
  which one a desk uses is its manifest's business.
- **LED and button**: the LED on **PF2** and the button on **PF3** (to
  ground; the pin is pulled up), each through a jumper. A second button
  resets.
- **Reset pin**: PF6, a GPIO at the factory. The board's reset button
  needs `SYSCFG0.RSTPINCFG` = RST, written once with
  `brio fuses <board> syscfg0=0xC8` (the factory 0xC0 with the reset
  field set; CRC off and EESAVE clear kept).
- **PD7 (VREFA)** carries **100 nF** to ground, the reference's
  decoupling. **PA7** carries nothing: CLKOUT and the TCD's WOD on its
  DEFAULT route run there (`avr_board.hpp`'s `board_pa7_free`).
- **Analog wires**: PD6 (the DAC's output) to PD1 and to PD7, and
  10 kOhm from the net to ground - one analog net of three pins with
  PD7's 100 nF on it, the AVR64EA48 board's net
  ([avr64ea48.md](avr64ea48.md), which measures the capacitor at 96 nF
  by its decay), the loop of `test_avr_analog` (`avr_board.hpp`'s
  `board_analog_net`, `board_dac_load_ohms`). The resistor is what a
  falling DAC step needs on this part: its buffer sinks about 20 uA
  ([../avrxt/dac.md](../avrxt/dac.md)).
- **Provisioning for the storage suites**: BOOTSIZE = 128 and CODESIZE
  = 0, as on the DB board ([../avrxt/nvm.md](../avrxt/nvm.md)), and a
  USERROW identity label ([../avrxt/userrow.md](../avrxt/userrow.md)).
- Manifest type `da48`; the project is `avrxt/`
  ([../avrxt/README.md](../avrxt/README.md)).
