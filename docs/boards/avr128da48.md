# An AVR128DA48 board

A self-built board for the 48-pin parts of three AVR families; its
silkscreen reads "AVRxxx[DA|DB|EA]48 / ATmega4809 - REV 1.2". This
assembly carries an AVR128DA48 (128 KB of flash, 16 KB of SRAM,
signature `1E 97 08`, `SYSCFG.REVID` 0x17), the DA of the `avrxt/`
stratum's bench ([../avrxt/README.md](../avrxt/README.md)); the same
PCB fitted with an AVR64EA48 is [avr64ea48.md](avr64ea48.md). The
design files are not in this repository; a repository of their own is
in preparation. What the board offers a program is an AVR DA with an
external clock it makes itself (the DA has no crystal oscillator), a
32 kHz crystal, an isolated console, a LED, a button and an analog net
the converter suite loops through.

## The board

- **Clock**: the DA has no crystal oscillator, so the board makes the
  high-frequency clock itself, a 24 MHz quartz and two single inverters,
  and feeds it to PA0 as EXTCLK; PA1 is then a GPIO. Solder jumpers
  (silkscreen "EXT CLK OPTS") choose among three arrangements: the
  quartz on PA0/PA1 for a DB's crystal oscillator, the inverter
  oscillator into PA0, or no clock and both pins free. A 32.768 kHz
  crystal sits on PF0/PF1. Both were measured running;
  [../avrxt/clkctrl.md](../avrxt/clkctrl.md) says how each is seen.
- **Supply**: a jumper selects VDD among 3.3 V, 5 V and an external
  supply through a Schottky diode, and a VDDIO2 selection that only a DB
  uses. Measured at 5.07 V by the probe.
- **Console**: USB-C to a CH340B behind a digital isolator ("ISO USB"),
  reaching USART2 ALT1, PF4 (TX) / PF5 (RX), through the "to UART"
  solder jumpers (the other position routes the two pins to the header);
  460800 baud in the console apps. This CH340B carries a USB serial
  number written into its EEPROM with WCH's CH34xSerCfg, so its console
  is addressed by `/dev/serial/by-id` like a probe's. Two cautions for
  whoever repeats that write: the tool's "disable the EEPROM
  configuration" box stays unticked, or the chip ignores the
  configuration the tool reads back; and never write 0x57 at EEPROM
  offset 0x03, which sets the EEPROM's permanent write protection.
- **Probe**: any UPDI probe avrdude drives, on the board's 10-pin
  "ATMEL ICE AVR" connector: an Atmel-ICE on its AVR port
  ([../probes/atmel-ice.md](../probes/atmel-ice.md)) or a PICkit 4 in
  its AVR mode (the mechanisms are
  [../probes/README.md](../probes/README.md)'s); which one a desk uses
  is its manifest's business.
- **LED and button**: the LED on PF2 and the button on PF3 (to ground,
  the pin pulled up), each through a jumper. A second button resets.
- **Identity and provisioning**: a USERROW label
  ([../avrxt/userrow.md](../avrxt/userrow.md)); BOOTSIZE = 128 and
  CODESIZE = 0 for the storage suites, as on the AVR128DB48 board
  ([../avrxt/nvm.md](../avrxt/nvm.md)).

## What a program meets on this board

- **The reset pin is PF6, a GPIO at the factory.** The board's reset
  button works only once `SYSCFG0.RSTPINCFG` = RST is written, once,
  with `brio fuses <board> syscfg0=0xC8` (the factory 0xC0 with the
  reset field set; CRC off and EESAVE clear kept).
- **PD7 (VREFA) carries 100 nF to ground**, the reference's decoupling.
  PA7 carries nothing: CLKOUT and the TCD's WOD on its DEFAULT route run
  there (`avr_board.hpp`'s `board_pa7_free`).
- **The analog net**: PD6 (the DAC's output) is wired to PD1 and to
  PD7, with 10 kOhm from the net to ground, one net of three pins with
  PD7's 100 nF on it. It is the AVR64EA48 board's net too
  ([avr64ea48.md](avr64ea48.md) measures the capacitor at 96 nF by its
  decay) and the loop of `test_avr_analog`; `avr_board.hpp` states it
  (`board_analog_net`, `board_dac_load_ohms`). The resistor is what a
  falling DAC step needs on this part: its buffer sinks about 20 uA
  ([../avrxt/dac.md](../avrxt/dac.md)).

## Flashing

`brio flash <board> <app>` writes the board through avrdude over UPDI;
the board type in the manifest is `da48`, and the project is `avrxt/`
([../avrxt/README.md](../avrxt/README.md)).
