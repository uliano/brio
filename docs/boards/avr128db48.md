# An AVR128DB48 board

A self-built board around an AVR128DB48 (48 pins, 128 KB of flash,
16 KB of SRAM), the bench chip of the `avrxt/` stratum
([../avrxt/README.md](../avrxt/README.md)). Its design files are not in
this repository; a repository of their own is in preparation. What the
board offers a brio program is a crystal-clocked AVR DB with a serial
console, a UPDI probe header and a USERROW label that tells it from its
siblings.

## The board

- **Clock**: a 24 MHz crystal on PA0/PA1, so those two pins are not
  GPIOs here; `Clock<ClockSource::crystal, 24'000'000>` is the board's
  clock type. No 32 kHz crystal: the RTC and the kernel tick run on the
  internal OSC32K, and PF0/PF1 are free.
- **Supply**: jumper-selectable, 3.3 V or 5 V. VDDIO2 is powered, so
  PORTC (the MVIO domain) is usable and could talk 3.3 V logic while the
  rest of the chip runs at 5 V.
- **Console**: a CH340 bridge on USART2 ALT1, PF4 (TX) / PF5 (RX),
  460800 baud in the console apps. The CH340 has no USB serial number,
  so the console is addressed by `/dev/serial/by-path`, the socket it
  is plugged into.
- **Probe**: an Atmel-ICE over UPDI on a 6-pin ISP header, pin 2 VCC,
  pin 5 UPDI, pin 6 GND ([../probes/atmel-ice.md](../probes/atmel-ice.md)).
- **Identity**: a label in the USERROW, written once over UPDI and
  printed by every test suite's banner
  ([../avrxt/userrow.md](../avrxt/userrow.md)).
- **LED**: on PF2 through a solder jumper, bridged on this board. PF2
  is the default LED pin of every self-built AVR board and the one the
  apps drive; the jumper is what makes it a LED, and not every board has
  it bridged.

## Flashing and provisioning

`brio flash <board> <app>` writes the board through avrdude over UPDI;
the board type in the manifest is `db48`. The same design fitted with
a 28- or 32-pin part would be `db28` or `db32`.

The fuses are provisioning, written over UPDI and surviving every
reflash. For the storage suites the board carries BOOTSIZE = 128 and
CODESIZE = 0, which puts all code in the BOOT section and leaves the
rest of the flash free for `NvHeap`; `brio fuses <board> bootsize=128`
writes it, and [../avrxt/nvm.md](../avrxt/nvm.md) says why.
