# WCH's CH32V003F4P6 evaluation board

WCH's own evaluation board (R0-1v1) for the CH32V003F4P6 (TSSOP20,
16 KB of flash, 2 KB of SRAM, the QingKe V2A core): the second and last
part of the `ch32v00x/` stratum, and the smallest silicon brio runs on
([../ch32v00x/README.md](../ch32v00x/README.md)). What the board offers
a brio program is that extreme: a core with no multiplier, a flash that
holds a suite only as group images, and a one-wire debug port with the
console on the probe's own serial.

The documents of record are the CH32V003 datasheet V1.6 and the
CH32V003 reference manual (WCH's site;
[../ch32v00x/vendor/README.md](../ch32v00x/vendor/README.md) says what
is on the desk). The silicon's identity: `misa` `0x40800014` (E and C,
no multiplier, WCH's own extension), ESIG FLACAP `0x0010` (16 KB), the
96-bit unique id in ESIG.

## The board

- **Clock**: HSI 24 MHz; the chip wakes at HSI/3 = 8 MHz (HPRE's reset
  value) and the kernel apps run the PLL at 48 MHz. A 24 MHz crystal Y1
  is soldered on PA1/PA2 (the board reference's note 8: R4 and R5
  unfitted, the pins the crystal's), so those two of the eighteen GPIOs
  are the HSE's.
- **Supply**: the board's own USB port (power only, 5 V behind S2)
  feeding the on-board LDO. As measured by the chip itself, the PVD
  reads not low at its 3.3 V level and low from the 3.5 V one, and
  VREFINT puts the rail at 3.27 V. Powered from the probe's 3V3 pin
  through the ribbon alone it sits near 3.0 V (the cable's drop), which
  is why the suites judge their supply against the PVD's bracket, the
  one witness a wireless board has, and never against a nominal 3.3 V;
  the platform, sleep and console suites run on it.
- **Debug**: PD1 = SWDIO alone (the V2A has the 1-wire SDI only) and
  GND, on a WCH-LinkE ([../probes/wch-link.md](../probes/wch-link.md)).
  The TSSOP20 pin numbers: PD1 is pin 18, PD5 pin 2, PD6 pin 3, PD7 pin
  4, PA1 pin 5, PA2 pin 6, PC0..PC7 pins 10..17, PD2 pin 19, PD3 pin 20,
  PD4 pin 1.
- **Console**: USART1 on PD5 (TX) / PD6 (RX), the default column, wired
  to the WCH-Link's TX/RX, so the probe's own serial port is the
  console, at 115200; the console prints `CH32V003 brio console`.
- **LEDs**: two, on the P4 row pins, reached by a jumper wire to any
  pad; no pad of the chip drives one by itself.
- **Reset button** S1 on PD7, which is the reset pin only when the
  option bytes' RST_MODE says so (the board reference's note 5).
- **Option bytes** as found: `0x08f75aa5`, which is RDPR `0xA5` (the
  flash unprotected) and USER `0xF7`.
- **Storage**: the image gets 15 KB; the top kilobyte is the
  NvJournal's attic of sixteen 64-byte pages
  ([../ch32v00x/nvm.md](../ch32v00x/nvm.md)). There is no heap on this
  array.
- **What it shipped with**: WCH's demo, running at first contact.

## What a program meets on this board

- **The pads free for a suite's jumpers**: PC0..PC7, PD0, PD2, PD3 and
  PD4. PD1 is the debug wire, PD5/PD6 the console, PD7 the reset
  button, PA1/PA2 the crystal.
- **A restart loop was seen once**, about twenty times a second with no
  byte sent and the probe unable to attach ("failed to connect with
  riscvchip"), and ended when the board's own USB was connected. Whether
  the rail had any supply at that moment was not recorded; a rail with
  none, fed through the pads' protection diodes, gives the CH32X035's
  board the same signature
  ([ch32x035-evt-f8u6.md](ch32x035-evt-f8u6.md)).

## Flashing

`brio flash <board> <app>` writes the board through WCH's OpenOCD fork
and the WCH-LinkE; the board type in the manifest is `v003f4`, the
project `ch32v00x/` and the preset `ch32v003f4-release`
([../ch32v00x/README.md](../ch32v00x/README.md)). On this board type a
suite with a groups line builds as one image per group
([README.md](README.md), "How a board joins the bench").
