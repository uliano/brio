# WCH's CH32V003F4P6 evaluation board

WCH's own evaluation board (R0-1v1) for the **CH32V003F4P6** (TSSOP20,
16 KB flash, 2 KB SRAM, the QingKe V2A core): the second and last part
of the `ch32v00x/` stratum, and the smallest silicon brio runs on.
Documents of record: the CH32V003 datasheet V1.6 and the CH32V003
reference manual (WCH's site - see
[../ch32v00x/vendor/README.md](../ch32v00x/vendor/README.md) for what
is on the desk). Silicon identity: `misa` `0x40800014` (E and C, no
multiplier, WCH's own extension), ESIG FLACAP `0x0010` (16 KB), the
96-bit unique id in ESIG.

- **Clock**: HSI 24 MHz; the chip wakes at HSI/3 = 8 MHz (HPRE's reset
  value) and the kernel apps run the PLL at 48 MHz. A 24 MHz crystal
  Y1 is SOLDERED on PA1/PA2 (the board reference's note 8: R4 and R5
  unfitted, the pins the crystal's), so those two of the eighteen
  GPIOs are the HSE's.
- **Supply**: the board's own USB port (power only, 5 V behind S2)
  feeding the on-board LDO - as measured by the chip itself, the PVD
  reads not low at its 3.3 V level and low from the 3.5 V one, and
  VREFINT puts it at 3.27 V. Powered from the probe's 3V3 pin through
  the ribbon alone it sits near 3.0 V (the cable's drop), which is why
  the suites judge their supply against the PVD's bracket, the one
  witness a wireless board has, and never against a nominal 3.3 V.
- **Debug**: PD1 = SWDIO alone - the V2A has the 1-wire SDI only -
  and GND, on the second WCH-LinkE of the desk
  ([../probes/wch-link.md](../probes/wch-link.md)). The TSSOP20 pin
  numbers: PD1 is pin 18, PD5 pin 2, PD6 pin 3, PD7 pin 4, PA1 pin 5,
  PA2 pin 6, PC0..PC7 pins 10..17, PD2 pin 19, PD3 pin 20, PD4 pin 1.
- **Console**: USART1 on PD5 (TX) / PD6 (RX), the default column,
  wired to the WCH-Link's TX/RX - the probe's own serial port is the
  console, at 115200, and `console` prints `CH32V003 brio console`.
- **LEDs**: two, on the P4 row pins - a jumper wire to any pad; no
  pad of the chip drives one by itself.
- **Reset button** S1 on PD7, which is the reset pin only when the
  option bytes' RST_MODE says so (the board reference's note 5).
- **Option bytes** as found: `0x08f75aa5` - RDPR `0xA5`, the flash
  unprotected; USER `0xF7`.
- **What it shipped with**: WCH's demo, running at first contact.
- **Storage**: the image gets 15 KB, the top kilobyte is the
  NvJournal's attic of sixteen 64-byte pages
  ([../ch32v00x/nvm.md](../ch32v00x/nvm.md)); there is no heap on this
  array.
- **The pads free for a suite's jumpers**: PC0..PC7, PD0, PD2, PD3 and
  PD4 (PD1 is the debug wire, PD5/PD6 the console, PD7 the reset
  button, PA1/PA2 the crystal).
- Manifest type `v003f4`; the project is `ch32v00x/`, preset
  `ch32v003f4-release` ([../ch32v00x/README.md](../ch32v00x/README.md)).
