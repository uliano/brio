# A CH32V006K8U6 module

A small module around WCH's CH32V006K8U6 (QFN32, 62 KB of flash, 8 KB
of SRAM, the QingKe V2C core), the RISC-V board of the bench and the
`ch32v00x/` stratum's larger part ([../ch32v00x/README.md](../ch32v00x/README.md)).
What the module offers a brio program is the smallest-class RISC-V core
brio runs on, with a crystal, a one-wire debug port and a console that
rides the probe's own serial port, so one cable carries everything. The
module has no silkscreen, so this page gives the QFN32 pin numbers of
every pad a suite uses.

The documents of record are the CH32V006 datasheet V2.0 and the
CH32V00X reference manual V1.5 (WCH's site; the local copies are in the
desk's document folder). The silicon's identity: ChipID `0x00600620`,
`misa` `0x40801014` (E, C, the multiply-only M, and WCH's own
extension), `marchid` `0xdc68d843`.

## The board

- **Clock**: HSI 24 MHz; the chip wakes at HSI/3 = 8 MHz (HPRE's reset
  value) and the kernel apps run the PLL at 48 MHz. A 24 MHz crystal on
  PA1/PA2: `test_ch32_clock` runs on it through the PLL, the other apps
  stay on the HSI.
- **Supply**: 3.3 V, taken from the probe's 3V3 pin.
- **Debug**: PD1 = SWDIO (the 1-wire SDI, the default), PB3 = SWCLK
  (the 2-wire mode), GND ([../probes/wch-link.md](../probes/wch-link.md)).
  The QFN32 pin numbers: PD1 is pin 23, PB3 pin 1, PD5 pin 27, PD6 pin
  28.
- **Console**: USART1 on PD5 (TX) / PD6 (RX), the default alternate
  function, wired to the WCH-Link's TX/RX, so the probe's own serial
  port is the console, at 115200. The same two pins are WCH's serial ISP
  path, the way back in should a program ever close the debug
  interface.
- **LED**: the module's LED has no pin of its own; it is jumpered, and
  every app of the project drives PC0 (pin 13): bonded on all five
  packages of the family, no bus and no analog function, TIM2_CH3 as
  its alternate function.
- **Reset pin**: PA7 on this package (the datasheet's note in 2.1), so
  PC5 and PD7 are ordinary pads here.
- **Option bytes** as found: `0x08f75aa5`, which is RDPR `0xA5` (the
  flash unprotected), USER `0xF7`, FLASH_WPR all ones (no unit
  protected).
- **Storage**: the image gets 40 KB; the top 22 KB are the NvHeap's and
  the NvJournal's ([../ch32v00x/nvm.md](../ch32v00x/nvm.md)), and
  `test_ch32_nvm` letter `w` wipes them.
- **What it shipped with**: WCH's demo, which prints
  `SystemClk:48000000` and `ChipID:00600620` on USART1 at reset; saved
  as a 62 KB image (4144 bytes used) before the first flash, in the
  desk's document folder.

## The jumpers the suites want

Each is probed by its suite, and each letter declines without it:

- PD2 to PD4 (pin 24 to pin 26) serves `test_ch32_tim`'s captures and
  `test_ch32_pin`'s levels, edges and scanner.
- PC6 to PC7 (pin 20 to pin 21) is `test_ch32_spi`'s loopback, off on
  a desk where PC5..PC7 and PC1 are wired to another board's SPI.
- `test_ch32_i2c` wants a peer board running `twi_peer` on PC2 (SCL,
  pin 15) and PC1 (SDA, pin 14) with the peer's pull-ups and a shared
  GND; this module has no pull-ups of its own. The peer runs at 3.3 V
  like the module: its pull-ups set the level the module's pads see,
  and a peer tied to other boards brings their rail onto the node too.
- PC4 (pin 18) carries the remap and ADC letters through its own pull,
  no wire; the LED is on PC0 (pin 13).
- USART2's column 4, PB0 (TX, pin 10) and PB1 (RX, pin 11), is the link
  `usart_peer` serves, and TIM1's CH2 and CH3 on the same two pads under
  its remap 0110 are its waveform engine; the crystal clocks it.

## Flashing

`brio flash <board> <app>` writes the module through WCH's OpenOCD fork
and a WCH-Link; the board type in the manifest is `v006k8`, and the
project is `ch32v00x/` ([../ch32v00x/README.md](../ch32v00x/README.md)).
